1. About Jynex

You must sign in with Google or with an email address and password to use Jynex. Keep your sign-in details secure and tell the project maintainer if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

3. Acceptable use

Use Jynex lawfully. Do not interfere with the service, attempt unauthorized access, or use it to distribute harmful or unlawful material. Follow the terms and policies of third-party services and websites you visit.

4. Search results and images

Search results, descriptions, and images may be incomplete, inaccurate, unavailable, or changed by their providers. Jynex does not control linked websites. Image reuse terms vary; check the license and attribution details on each Wikimedia Commons file page before reusing an image.

5. Availability

Jynex and its third-party services are provided on an “as available” basis. Features may change or stop working. Nothing in these terms excludes rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

6. Data and privacy

Firebase Authentication processes your email address and account credentials to sign you in. Your acceptance of these terms is saved with your Firebase account and checked before the app opens. Recent searches and saved links are stored in Firestore under your account so they can follow you across devices. Your theme preference is stored in this browser. Search requests are sent to Purili; image searches are sent to Wikimedia Commons. View the Privacy Policy.

7. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated when the service changes. If a material update requires new acceptance, Jynex will ask you to accept the updated version before continuing. Contact the Jynex maintainer through the repository where you accessed the service.
