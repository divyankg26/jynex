Your information

Privacy Policy.

This policy describes the information Jynex handles in the current version of the app, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: November 20, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies when you visit the Jynex site, including its landing, information, and search-app pages. Jynex is a search interface. It does not crawl the web or maintain its own web index.

2. Information Jynex handles

Account information

When you sign in with email and password or Google, Firebase Authentication processes account identifiers such as your email address, Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, and email-verification state. Firebase also handles sign-in sessions and the email messages used for address verification and password resets.

Terms acceptance

Jynex stores the accepted Terms of Service version and acceptance time in Firestore, associated with your Firebase user ID. This lets Jynex check your acceptance when you return.

Search history and saved pages

Recent searches—including the query, any filters, and the time searched—and pages you save—including the page URL, title, available description, source query, and save time—are stored in Firestore under your account. Jynex uses this data to show your history and Library and to sync them when you sign in on another device. The app’s Firestore rules limit access to the signed-in account owner.

Search requests

For web search, suggestions, and available quick-answer cards, Jynex sends your query and relevant request details to Purili. For image search, it sends your image query to Wikimedia Commons. When you open a result, your browser connects to that destination. These providers and destination sites operate independently and handle request data under their own policies.

Browser storage

Jynex stores your light or dark theme preference and a short verification-email resend cooldown in your browser. Firebase Authentication also maintains your sign-in session using browser storage mechanisms. After a successful migration to Firestore, search history and saved pages are no longer kept in local browser storage as the primary copy.

3. How information is used

Jynex uses account information to sign you in and enforce account access; stores your Terms acceptance to remember it; sends queries to the selected search provider; and stores history and saved pages so Library features work across devices. The current app code does not initialize Google Analytics.

4. Service providers and links

  • Google Firebase provides Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Review Firebase privacy and security information and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Purili supplies Jynex web search, suggestions, favicons, and any available quick-answer content. Review Purili’s Privacy Policy.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies image search results. Review the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Review GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

When you follow an external link, that provider or destination site may receive information from your browser, such as the requested page and ordinary connection metadata. Jynex does not control those services.

5. Retention and your choices

You can remove an individual recent search from the homepage, suggestions, or Library; clear all recent searches from Account; and remove saved pages from Library. These actions update the account’s Firestore library. Signing out does not delete your account data. Jynex does not currently provide an in-app account deletion control. For an account or data deletion request, contact the project maintainer through the Jynex GitHub repository; do not post passwords or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Requests to Firebase, Purili, Wikimedia, GitHub, or a linked site about information they handle must be directed to that provider.

6. Security

Jynex uses Firebase Authentication and account-scoped Firestore rules to protect account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in method private and sign out on devices you do not control.

7. Children and changes

Jynex is a general web search interface and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact the project maintainer through the repository above. This policy may be updated as Jynex changes; the effective date at the top will be revised when it is updated.

This page describes Jynex’s current data practices. It is not legal advice. Please also review the policies of each third-party service linked above.
