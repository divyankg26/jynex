Help center

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you get more out of Jynex.

What is Jynex?

Jynex is a search interface for web results and images, with filters, recent searches, and a library for saved pages. See About Jynex for more.

Does Jynex have its own web index?

No. Jynex displays pages indexed by Purili’s independent search service. Jynex does not crawl or index websites itself, and it does not manually add websites to an index.

Why do search results come from another service?

Purili supplies the web search results and suggestions shown inside Jynex. Jynex provides the interface, search tools, and account library; the third-party service supplies the index and result data. Search results may change or be unavailable.

How do I search for an exact phrase or a particular file?

Open Filters below the search box. You can enter an exact phrase, restrict results to a website, or choose PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, or CSV. You can also type supported operators such as site:example.com.

How do suggestions and recent searches work?

Suggestions are requested from Purili as you type. When the search box is empty, Jynex can show recent searches saved to your account. Select one to run it again, or use the × beside it to delete it.

How do I return to the homepage?

Click the Jynex logo in the top-left corner. It clears the current results, closes open panels, and returns the page to the top of the homepage.

Where are my search history and saved pages stored?

They are stored in Firestore under your Jynex account. Sign in with the same account on another device to load them there. The light or dark theme preference is stored only in the current browser.

How do I remove searches or saved pages?

Delete an individual recent search with its × button in the Library, homepage list, or search suggestions. Use Clear all search history in Account to remove all recent searches. Remove a saved page from the Library’s Saved tab or toggle its star on the result.

How do I search images?

Run a search and choose the Images tab. Image results are supplied by Wikimedia Commons. Open an image’s source page to review its license and attribution details before reusing it.

Why must I verify my email or accept the Terms?

Email-and-password accounts must verify their address before using the app. Google accounts do not need a separate verification step. All accounts must accept the Terms of Service before entering Jynex; acceptance is remembered with the account.

How do I change my password?

In the Account panel, choose Send password reset email. Follow the secure link sent to your email. If you use Google sign-in, manage your password through your Google Account.

Can I use Jynex without signing in?

No. Signing in is required to use search and to connect your search library to your account.

What happens if I refresh while viewing search results?

Jynex returns to the homepage after a refresh. Your recent search remains in your account history, so you can run it again from the homepage, suggestions, or Library.

What should I do if my library does not sync?

Check your internet connection and try again. Jynex needs access to your account’s Firestore library. If it still does not sync, the site owner may need to check the Firebase project’s Firestore availability and security rules.

For information on what account and search data Jynex handles, read the Privacy Policy.
