About

A search space for curiosity.

Jynex brings web search, image discovery, and a personal library together in one focused interface. It is designed to make exploring the web feel clear and welcoming.

What Jynex does

Search for web pages and browse the results in Jynex. Refine a search by exact phrase, website, or file type; explore image results; and keep useful searches and pages in your account library. A browser-style start page helps you pick up where you left off or find a new topic to explore.

Jynex is a search interface. It does not crawl the web or maintain its own index. Web pages, suggestions, and any available quick answers come from Purili’s independent search service. Images come from Wikimedia Commons. Jynex presents the results from these providers in its own interface.

Built around your account

Jynex uses Firebase Authentication for email-and-password and Google sign-in. Email accounts must verify their address before entering the app. Users must accept the Terms of Service before using search. Recent searches and saved pages are kept with the signed-in account in Firestore; the display theme is kept in the browser.

Designed to stay out of the way

The interface adapts to mobile and desktop, supports light and dark themes, includes keyboard shortcuts, and respects reduced-motion preferences. Search results open in separate tabs so Jynex remains ready when you return.

For help understanding features or account behavior, visit the FAQ. For information about data and providers, read the Privacy Policy.
